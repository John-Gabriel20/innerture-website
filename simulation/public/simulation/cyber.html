Mission Brief

The Problem: Our security team has detected a sophisticated "Social Engineering" attack targeting our employees. A fake IT support person is calling staff members, claiming there's an urgent system issue and requesting their login credentials over the phone.

The Constraint: We cannot block every phone number in the world or implement a universal call-blocking system without disrupting legitimate business communications.

Manager Persona: Sarah Chen, CTO - "This is a human vulnerability, not a technical one. We need a strategic response that empowers our people instantly. Lead this initiative."

Strategic Response Protocol

As the Lead Security Analyst, you must deploy an immediate, clever solution to train and alert employees without traditional security awareness training sessions.

The Prompt

Suggest one clever way to "train" or "alert" employees instantly so they don't fall for social engineering attacks like this. Focus on an innovative, low-effort method that can be implemented immediately across the organization.

Success Criteria

  • Practical and implementable within hours
  • Doesn't require extensive training sessions
  • Addresses the human element of security
  • Can be deployed organization-wide quickly
