PROJECT AETHER: TRAVEL APP v2.0

The Situation

We just launched a flight-booking app specifically designed for seniors. Our analytics show a shocking discovery: 40% of users make it all the way to the final payment screen, then suddenly close the app without completing their purchase.

The Complication

We conducted user interviews to understand the drop-off. The feedback was heartbreaking but illuminating: senior users told us they are "scared" to click the 'Pay' button because it looks too aggressive and intimidating. Even worse, they aren't sure if their seat is actually reserved yet—they're terrified they'll lose their booking or get charged twice.

The Goal

Without writing new code or changing pricing, how can you change the 'Vibe' of the final payment screen to make it feel safe, clear, and encouraging? Transform anxiety into confidence.

Strategic Challenge: Changing the Vibe

Your task is to redesign the user's emotional experience at the critical moment of purchase—not through code changes, but through visual and textual psychology.

Context & Constraints

  • Target Demographic: Users aged 60+, less tech-savvy, risk-averse
  • Current Pain Points: Aggressive call-to-action button, unclear confirmation messaging, fear of double-charging
  • Budget: Zero code changes. Copy, layout, and visual styling only.
  • Timeline: Must be implementable within one business day

The Design Prompt

Redesign the payment screen's emotional tone and messaging to address senior users' fears. Consider: What would make you feel safe and confident? What words rebuild trust? How can the page layout communicate "your booking is secure"?

Resource Library

User Testing Insights

Senior User Feedback: "Too scary to click", "What if I get charged twice?", "Where's my confirmation?"

Pain Point Analysis: Aggressive CTA design, missing reassurance messaging, unclear reservation status

Psychological Design Principles

Trust Building: Clear confirmation, reassuring language, visual safety cues

Call-to-Action Psychology: Soft vs. aggressive language, button color psychology, proximity anxiety reduction

Reference UI Patterns

Examples: Banking interfaces (trust-focused), retirement planning apps, healthcare platforms

Key Elements: Two-step confirmation, progress indicators, reassurance text, accessible typography

Copy Library

Current: "Complete Payment" | Trustworthy alternatives: "Secure Your Seat", "Confirm & Protect Your Booking"

Reassurance Messaging: "Your seat is reserved", "Your payment is encrypted", "Final step—you're safe"
